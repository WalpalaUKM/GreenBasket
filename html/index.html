Our Story & Guarantee

100% Biological Produce Direct From Sri Lankan Farmer Cooperatives

GreenBasket was founded with a clear mission: connect verified sustainable smallholders across Nuwara Eliya, Weligama, and Jaffna directly with conscious families in Sri Lanka. 100% PGS certified biological cultivation with zero chemical weedicides or artificial wax coatings.

5:00 AM Morning Harvest

Produce is harvested at dawn and transferred into chilled crates within 2 hours to seal in natural vitamins and crunch.

Fair Pricing for 45+ Farming Families

We guarantee 20% higher returns paid directly to local farmer cooperatives, supporting sustainable rural livelihoods.

Central Cold Hub & Islandwide Delivery

Central quality-sorting and temperature-controlled doorstep packaging ensure farm freshness delivered straight to your home.
