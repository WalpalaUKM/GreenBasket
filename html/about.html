Rooted in Matara, Sri Lanka

Cultivating Wholesome Living
From Southern Sri Lanka

GreenBasket is Sri Lanka’s premier direct-from-farm organic cooperative headquartered in historic Matara City. We empower over 45 regenerative southern farming families, providing our community with pesticide-free heirloom rice, virgin coconut oil, sun-dried Ceylon spices, and pristine river-valley produce.

Our Matara Heritage

Direct From Soil to Your Table in Under 12 Hours

Conventional supermarket produce languishes in cold chemical storage for weeks. At GreenBasket, our harvesting cycle is coordinated with customer orders daily at dawn across the lush Nilwala river plains and Akuressa plantations. Fresh greens, jackfruit, organic coconuts, and Ceylon Alba cinnamon reach our Matara hub and your doorstep the very same day.

Zero chemical weedicides, synthetic hormones, or artificial ripeners

Our Organic Principles

Every harvest is verified to protect both human health and delicate Sri Lankan ecosystems.

Heirloom Varieties

Preserving ancient Sri Lankan rice strains like Suwandel, Kalu Heenati, and Pachchaperumal which are rich in natural antioxidants and low glycemic index.

Fair Trade Sourcing

By cutting out exploitative middlemen and wholesale cartels, our farmers earn up to 40% higher returns, funding school education and community water sanitation.

Soil Regeneration

We enrich Southern Sri Lankan topsoil with biochar, nitrogen-fixing green manure, and compost, reversing erosion and maintaining groundwater purity.

Eco Packaging

100% biodegradable palm leaf cartons, compostable banana-leaf wrapping, and reusable glass bottles for oils and artisanal Kithul treacle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Where does GreenBasket source its fresh produce?
A: All produce comes directly from verified southern smallholders in Matara, Nilwala Valley, Galle, and Deniyaya adhering strictly to PGS biological farming with zero synthetic sprays.
Q: How does distance-based shipping work?
A: Shipping is calculated directly from our Matara Shop. Within 1 km, shipping is FREE on orders over Rs. 2,000 (otherwise Rs. 60). Deliveries 1-5 km are Rs. 100, 5-25 km are Rs. 180, and regional courier is Rs. 250-350.
Q: Can I pay with Cash on Delivery (COD)?
A: Yes! You can pay safely via cash or mobile banking when receiving your fresh produce box at your doorstep.
